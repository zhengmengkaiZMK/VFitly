# Quick Start

This guide helps you create your first VFitly virtual try-on result in a few minutes.

## Before you begin

Prepare:

- a clear person photo, ideally front-facing;
- a clothing image or a product URL;
- a VFitly account.

Sign in from [Login](/login), then open your [Dashboard](/dashboard).

## Option A: Try on from a clothing image

1. Open [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on).
2. Upload the person photo.
3. Upload or select the clothing item.
4. Choose the try-on mode available in the interface.
5. Generate the result.
6. Open [History](/dashboard/history) to review the output.

This workflow is best when you already have a product image, catalog image, or saved clothing reference.

## Option B: Try on from a shopping link

1. Copy a product URL from Amazon, eBay, Temu, Shopify, SHEIN, or another product page.
2. Open [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
3. Paste the URL and extract the product image when available.
4. Select the person photo.
5. Generate and review the try-on output.

If a page cannot be extracted automatically, save the product image manually and use [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on).

## Save useful items

After generating a promising result, open [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe) to keep reusable clothing items. This makes future try-on sessions faster.

## Create a 360° try-on video

When a still image is not enough, use the 360° video workflow from an eligible result. Free accounts include one 360° try-on video per account. Paid plans include monthly video allowances. See [Pricing](/pricing) for current limits.
