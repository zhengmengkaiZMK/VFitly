# Getting Started With VFitly

VFitly is designed around one goal: make fashion decisions easier to visualize before purchase, publishing, or product presentation.

## Main pages

### Dashboard

The [Dashboard](/dashboard) is your starting point. It gives quick access to try-on tools, wardrobe assets, history, and account usage.

### AI Clothes Changer

Open [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on) when you want to combine a person photo with a clothing image. This is the primary virtual try-on workflow.

### Product Try-On

Open [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on) when your starting point is a shopping link from Amazon, eBay, Temu, Shopify, SHEIN, or another product page.

### Wardrobe

Open [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe) to store reusable clothing items and keep your best assets organized.

### History

Open [History](/dashboard/history) to review past generations and compare results.

### Pricing

Open [Pricing](/pricing) to compare credits, monthly allowances, and 360° try-on video limits.

## Recommended workflow

For most users:

1. Start from [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on) or [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
2. Generate several still-image previews.
3. Keep useful items in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).
4. Use [History](/dashboard/history) to compare outputs.
5. Create a 360° try-on video only for your strongest candidates.

## Content quality tips

Use clear source images for better results:

- Person photo: front-facing, well-lit, minimal obstruction.
- Clothing image: complete item boundaries, clean background if possible.
- Product URL: use a direct product page rather than a search-result page.
