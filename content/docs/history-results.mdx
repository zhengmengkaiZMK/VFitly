# History and Results

[History](/dashboard/history) stores your generated try-on outputs so you can compare previous results and return to useful ideas later.

## What History is for

Use History to:

- review past AI clothes changer results;
- compare multiple product candidates;
- revisit 360° try-on video outputs;
- decide which items should be kept in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).

## Recommended workflow

1. Generate several previews from [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on) or [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
2. Open [History](/dashboard/history).
3. Compare visual results side by side over time.
4. Keep the best clothing assets in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).
5. Use strong still-image results as candidates for 360° video generation.

## Tips

- Generate multiple still-image options before using video quota.
- Delete or ignore weak results to keep your decision process simple.
- Use History as a comparison layer, not just a storage page.
