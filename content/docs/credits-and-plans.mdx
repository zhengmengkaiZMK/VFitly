# Credits and Plans

VFitly uses credits and plan limits to manage AI generation usage. You can compare current plans on [Pricing](/pricing).

## Credits

Credits are used for AI try-on generation. Different workflows may consume different amounts of credits depending on the generation type shown in the product interface.

Use credits for:

- [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on);
- [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on);
- outfit experiments and other supported generation flows.

## 360° video allowances

360° try-on videos require additional processing, so each plan has specific limits.

- Free accounts include 1 free 360° try-on video per account.
- Plus includes a monthly 360° video allowance.
- Ultra includes a higher monthly 360° video allowance.

For the latest exact numbers, visit [Pricing](/pricing).

## Choosing a plan

Choose Free if you want to test VFitly lightly.

Choose Plus if you regularly generate AI try-on previews and need more monthly credits.

Choose Ultra if you create higher-volume fashion content, compare many products, or need more 360° videos.
