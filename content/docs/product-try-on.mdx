# Product Try-On From Shopping Links

[Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on) is designed for products you discover online. Instead of manually collecting product images from different stores, you can paste a shopping link and move into the virtual try-on workflow.

## Supported starting points

You can try product pages from marketplaces and stores such as:

- Amazon;
- eBay;
- Temu;
- Shopify stores;
- SHEIN;
- other product pages with accessible product imagery.

Extraction availability depends on the page structure and whether the product image can be accessed. If extraction fails, manually save the product image and use [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on).

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Copy the product URL.
2. Open [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
3. Paste the URL.
4. Extract the product image when available.
5. Upload or select the person photo.
6. Generate the virtual try-on preview.
7. Review results in [History](/dashboard/history).
8. Save reusable items in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).

## Best practices

- Use direct product detail pages, not search result pages.
- Prefer pages with clear product images.
- Avoid product pages where the main image is hidden behind login or region restrictions.
- Compare several candidates before deciding which item to buy.
