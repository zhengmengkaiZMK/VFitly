A still image is often enough to judge color, basic shape, and overall style. But some fashion decisions need more context. A jacket may look different from the side. A dress may depend on silhouette. A full outfit may need a more dynamic preview.

That is where VFitly's 360° try-on video workflow becomes useful.

## Still image vs. 360° preview

Use a still try-on image when you want to:

- test many items quickly;
- compare colors or patterns;
- create a simple before-and-after preview;
- decide whether an item is worth deeper evaluation.

Use a 360° try-on video when you want to:

- present a more complete fashion concept;
- evaluate an outfit from more visual angles;
- prepare richer content for marketing or social posts;
- show a product idea with more movement than a static image.

You can begin with [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on), then use video generation when a look is worth presenting in more detail.

## Best practices for better results

For stronger 360° try-on outputs:

1. Use a clear full-body or half-body person image.
2. Choose product images with visible clothing boundaries.
3. Avoid heavily cropped or low-resolution product photos.
4. Save strong candidates in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).
5. Review final outputs in [History](/dashboard/history).
