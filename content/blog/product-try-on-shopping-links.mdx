Fashion discovery often starts outside your wardrobe. You may find a jacket on Amazon, a dress on SHEIN, a store product page on Shopify, or a trending item on Temu or eBay. The challenge is not finding products. The challenge is deciding which product is worth buying.

[VFitly Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on) helps bridge that gap by letting you start from a product URL and move quickly into an AI virtual try-on workflow.

## Why product-link try-on is useful

Marketplace pages are optimized for browsing, not personal visualization. A product may look great on a model but still feel uncertain for your own use case.

With VFitly, product-link try-on helps you:

- test items discovered on Amazon, eBay, Temu, Shopify stores, SHEIN, and other shopping pages;
- avoid manually downloading and organizing every product image;
- compare products against the same person photo;
- save better candidates into your [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).

## Recommended workflow

Use this flow when you discover a product online:

1. Copy the product URL from the shopping page.
2. Open [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
3. Paste the URL and let VFitly extract the product image when available.
4. Select or upload a person photo.
5. Generate a try-on result and compare it in [History](/dashboard/history).

If extraction is unavailable for a page, you can still use [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on) by uploading a product image manually.

## Who benefits most

Product-link try-on is valuable for:

- online shoppers comparing several stores;
- fashion affiliates preparing visual recommendations;
- Shopify merchants checking how catalog items appear in lifestyle contexts;
- content creators building outfit ideas from marketplace trends.

Instead of relying only on product thumbnails, you can bring candidates into one consistent visual workflow.

## Start with one link

Open [VFitly Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on), paste a product link, and generate your first preview. For repeated use, store your best items in [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe) and revisit finished results in [History](/dashboard/history).
