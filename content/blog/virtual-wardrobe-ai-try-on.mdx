A good virtual try-on workflow should not force you to upload the same clothing image again and again. If an item is useful, it should be saved, reused, compared, and organized.

That is why [VFitly Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe) is a core part of the product. It turns one-off try-on experiments into a reusable fashion workspace.

## What a virtual wardrobe solves

Without a wardrobe, AI try-on quickly becomes messy:

- product images are scattered across downloads and screenshots;
- favorite items are hard to find again;
- comparing old and new outfit ideas takes too much time;
- creators cannot maintain a consistent library of usable items.

A virtual wardrobe keeps your best clothing assets in one place so you can generate new looks faster.

## How to use VFitly Wardrobe

A simple workflow:

1. Generate a result in [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on) or [Product Try-On](/dashboard/product-try-on).
2. Save useful clothing items or generated assets to [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe).
3. Reuse the same item with different person photos.
4. Review previous outputs in [History](/dashboard/history).
5. Remove items you no longer need to keep the workspace clean.

## Practical use cases

The wardrobe is useful when you are:

- planning seasonal outfits;
- comparing similar dresses, jackets, or tops;
- preparing product visuals for social content;
- testing marketplace products before buying;
- building a repeatable catalog for an online store.

## Make try-on faster over time

The more you use VFitly, the more valuable your wardrobe becomes. Instead of restarting from scattered files, you can open [Wardrobe](/dashboard/wardrobe), pick an item, and continue the creative process.

For your next session, start with [AI Clothes Changer](/dashboard/try-on), then save the items that are worth reusing.
