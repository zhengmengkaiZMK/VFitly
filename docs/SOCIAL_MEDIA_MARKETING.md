### 正文 (Body)

```markdown
Hey everyone! 👋

I'm excited (and a bit nervous) to share something I've been working on for the past few months.

## The Problem That Started It All

Last year, I had what I thought was a brilliant SaaS idea. I spent 2 months building it, only to discover... nobody actually wanted it. The "problem" I was solving? Turns out, it wasn't really a problem.

That failure taught me something crucial: **You can't build a successful product without understanding real user pain points.**

## My "Aha" Moment

I started manually scrolling through Reddit and X (Twitter), reading hundreds of posts where people complained about existing tools. It was eye-opening! I found patterns I never would have discovered otherwise.

But here's the thing – it was incredibly time-consuming. I'd spend 4-5 hours just to analyze one keyword thoroughly.

So I thought: "What if I could automate this?"

## What I Built

Meet **VFitly** – an AI-powered tool that:

1. 🔍 **Searches Reddit & X** for discussions about any topic/product
2. 🤖 **Analyzes with AI** to identify patterns and frustrations
3. 📊 **Generates a structured report** with:
   - Executive summary of main pain points
   - Frustration score (0-100)
   - Top 6 pain point insights with severity levels
   - Real user quotes
   - Actionable improvement opportunities

**Example**: Type "Notion" → Get a detailed analysis of what users love and hate about it → Spot opportunities for your competing product.

## The Technical Journey

Building this was both exciting and challenging:

**Challenges I faced:**
- Integrating multiple APIs (Reddit search + AI analysis)
- Handling rate limits and API costs
- Making the AI output actually useful (not generic fluff)
- Designing an intuitive UI that doesn't overwhelm users

**What worked:**
- Using Server-Sent Events for real-time progress updates
- Structured AI prompts that generate actionable insights
- Adding export features (PDF, copy report)
- Implementing user authentication and quota systems

## Features I'm Proud Of

✅ **Dual Platform Search** – Both Reddit & X in one search
✅ **AI-Powered Analysis** – Not just keyword matching, real understanding
✅ **History Tracking** – Save and revisit your analyses
✅ **Export Options** – PDF reports and copyable text
✅ **Bilingual** – Full English/Chinese support
✅ **Free Tier** – 3 searches for guests, 5/day for registered users

## Who Is This For?

If you're:
- 🚀 Validating a startup idea
- 💼 A PM researching competitor pain points
- 🛠️ Building a product and want to know what users actually need
- 📊 Doing market research without expensive tools

Then this might help you save hours of manual research.
```

## 📊 效果追踪

### 关键指标

记录以下数据来优化营销策略：

- **访问量**: 帖子发布后的网站访问量
- **注册量**: 新用户注册数
- **使用量**: 实际使用搜索功能的次数
- **互动率**: 评论、点赞、分享数
- **转化率**: 从访问到注册的转化率

### A/B 测试

在不同 subreddit 测试：
- **不同标题** - 记录哪个标题获得更多点击
- **不同 CTA** - "Try it free" vs "Check it out" vs "Would love feedback"
- **不同长度** - 长文 vs 短文效果对比

## ⚠️ 注意事项

### Reddit 规则

1. **查看各 sub 规则** - 每个 subreddit 都有独特规则
2. **不要刷屏** - 同一内容不要在多个 sub 同时发布
3. **真诚互动** - 回复所有评论，不要只是发了就走
4. **标注自推** - 诚实说明这是你自己的项目
5. **遵守 10:1 规则** - 每发 1 次自推，至少要有 10 次其他有价值的互动

### 最佳发帖时间

**Reddit**:
- 美东时间周二-周四上午 8-11 点
- 美东时间周一、周三晚上 7-9 点

**X (Twitter)**:
- 美东时间周一-周五中午 12-1 点
- 美东时间周三下午 5-6 点
